# SentinelX 🛡️
### Digital Safety Intelligence Platform

SentinelX is a digital safety and cybersecurity platform designed to help identify online scams, detect suspicious social media profiles, analyze potentially malicious URLs, and recognize banking fraud risks.

## Features

- **Fake Social Media Account Detection** — Identify potential signs of fake profiles and impersonation.
- **Scam Call & OTP Fraud Detection** — Analyze suspicious messages and identify common scam indicators.
- **Phishing Link & Website Detection** — Examine URLs for potential phishing indicators.
- **Banking Fraud Risk Analysis** — Identify suspicious patterns in financial transactions.
- **Unified Threat Intelligence Dashboard** — View security alerts, threat information, and risk indicators.
- **AI-Powered Safety Assistant** — Provide accessible cybersecurity guidance and safety recommendations.
